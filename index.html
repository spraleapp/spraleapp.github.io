    Before you start

    Turn on a dictionary.

    Sprale uses the dictionary built into your iPhone, so meanings show up instantly, even offline. If tapping a word shows no definition, download one:

    1. 1

      Open the Settings app

    2. 2

      Go to General › Dictionary

    3. 3

      Tap a dictionary to download it, such as New Oxford American Dictionary, and wait for the tick

    Help

    Questions, answered.

    Still stuck? Email {{ site.contact_email }} and we’ll get back to you.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. Sprale works straight away with no sign-up, email or password. It creates an anonymous ID behind the scenes so your dictionary, bookmarks and progress are kept safe.

    Where do the definitions come from?

    From the dictionaries built into iOS. Sprale doesn’t send the words you tap to a third-party dictionary service.

    I tap a word but no meaning appears.

    Your iPhone probably doesn’t have a dictionary downloaded yet. Follow the setup steps above, then come back to Sprale.

    Can I stop words being added automatically?

    Yes. Open Settings in Sprale (the gear on the Today screen) and turn off auto-adding. You can then use + to save a word yourself.

    How do I remove a word or a highlight?

    Long-press the highlighted word in a reading and choose Remove Highlight or Remove from Dictionary.

    What do “Feel Confident” and “Needed help” mean?

    When you finish a reading without looking anything up, tap Feel Confident. If you looked up any words, you’ll see Continue instead, and that reading is marked Needed help in your library so you can revisit it.

    Why is it called Sprale?

    It comes from two German words: Sprache (language) and Lernen (learn).
